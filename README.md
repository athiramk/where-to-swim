# Where to Swim

**Which beach near South Melbourne is best for a swim right now?**

Where to Swim looks at the latest weather, sea, tide and daylight and ranks 11 beaches, best first, so you can decide where to go.

## What it does

- Ranks the beaches, each marked **Great, Good, Fair or Poor** with a score out of 100 and a short reason.
- Shows wind, waves, air temperature, rain, sunset and the next tides.
- Marks a beach down after dark, in thunderstorms, during official weather warnings, after heavy rain and in very strong gusts.
- Three beaches are only worth visiting around low tide: Jawbone, Ricketts Point and Mushroom Reef. For those it shows the next low tide in daylight and the best time to go.
- Tap any beach for more detail and local tips from Parks Victoria and other published sources.
- Updates about every 30 minutes and shows when it last updated.
- Can be added to your phone's home screen, and still shows the last information it had when you have no signal.

## The beaches

Sandridge, Port Melbourne, South Melbourne, Middle Park, Albert Park, St Kilda, Elwood, Half Moon Bay, Jawbone Marine Sanctuary, Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary and Mushroom Reef Marine Sanctuary.

## Please note

- It's a guide only. Always check the flags, lifeguards and signs before you swim.
- Water quality is not included. Each beach links to EPA Victoria's Beach Report, so check it before you swim.
- Weather, sea, tide and warning information comes from the Australian Bureau of Meteorology. This is a personal project and isn't connected to or endorsed by the Bureau, Parks Victoria or EPA Victoria.
